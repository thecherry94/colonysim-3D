Uses the slice view: everything above the chosen floor is hidden. PgUp/PgDn also work.

Controls

  • Camera tool: left-drag orbits, right-drag pans, wheel zooms
  • Build tool: left-drag on the ground draws an area. Right-drag orbits, middle-drag pans.
  • Doors and windows: click a wall of the ghost. Inside walls: drag a line inside it. Stairs: drag from the bottom step the way they should climb.
