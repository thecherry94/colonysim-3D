Tools

Controls

  • Change level: PgUp/PgDn, Shift+wheel, or the slider
  • Jump to a spot: double-click a block (Camera tool)
  • Use a tool: left-drag a rectangle. It acts on the current view level. At the top level it acts on whatever surface you click.
  • Make it 3D: while still holding the mouse, press PgDn (dig deeper) or PgUp (build walls taller), then release
  • Walls go on the outline of the rectangle, so one drag makes a room. Floors fill it.
  • Camera with a tool active: right-drag orbits, middle-drag pans, wheel zooms
